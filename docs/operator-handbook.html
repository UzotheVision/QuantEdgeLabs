Giving people access

Signup is invite-only. Each person gets a one-time code; they sign up at quantedgelabs.net with their email and that code. Create more codes at /admin → Beta Invites, or ask Claude to mint a batch.

Never share the admin access code. It logs straight into your own account. Friends with their own accounts keep separate books, journals and settings.

Shipping a change

The 512MB droplet can't build the app, so changes are built on the laptop and copied up. Skipping the copy is exactly how the site once showed 4 ideas while the engine had 12.

npm run build
scp -i ~/.ssh/quantedge_deploy -r dist root@104.248.127.195:/opt/quantedge/
ssh -i ~/.ssh/quantedge_deploy root@104.248.127.195 'cd /opt/quantedge && git pull && pm2 restart quantedge-web'

After the 2GB resize the droplet can build for itself, and this becomes one command.

The rules the engine is built on

Measured, not inferred. Every idea comes from a scanner that measured something — flow at the ask, a dated bottom, a real pullback. No invented levels.

Coherent with itself. A card may not contradict its own theory or its own tape. Opposing flow subtracts points.

Live, not carried forward. Anything shown as current is recomputed now or labelled with its age. Publish-time numbers are history, not the present.

Long and short on equal footing. Shorts clear the same measured-evidence bar as longs; opposing tape subtracts in both directions; a tape flip retires the stale opposite-side card.

Judged by the record. Every card's outcome is tracked; scanners that don't pay get demoted by their own numbers.

Stale-value audit (Sep 24)

A code audit found 15 places where an old number could be shown as current. The ones that could cause a bad entry or wrong size are fixed; the rest are queued.
